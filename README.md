# EX No.4.1. CUT SECTION OF SOLIDS
## DATE:

## AIM
To create the solid model from given drawing and section the solid model

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3.16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:

1.Open autodesk fusion 360 and select create sketch and choose the xy plane

<img width="507" height="568" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-08 233147" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f361186a-69fd-4cad-9dda-cb1743843e99" />

2.Draw a central 50 mm circle, two 30 mm circles spaced 35 mm to the left and right of the center, and connecting them with tangent lines.

<img width="363" height="461" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0b6fb840-f5e7-490c-b859-342388ca857f" />

3.Add the two 12 mm mounting holes inside that same sketch, making them concentric with the outer 30 mm circles.

4.Press finish sketch ans Extrude the base flange profile upwards by 10 mm.

<img width="503" height="605" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-03 211338" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4e23729e-471d-4d40-ae59-cb3fb13ee8e3" />

5.Extrude the central 50 mm cylinder upwards by 28 mm using the "Join" operation (make sure to unhide your first sketch in the browser tree to select it).

6.Press create sketch and choose the top surface of the cylinder and Sketch the concentric bore circles (30 mm and 20 mm) on the top flat face of the cylinder.

7.Cut the 20 mm hole all the way through the bottom of the part using the Extrude tool.

<img width="503" height="605" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-03 211338" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ca04287c-30e6-4a06-9546-c8cea6f871db" />

8.Cut the 30 mm counterbore down by a distance of 6 mm.


9.Sketch a 6 mm circle on the vertical XZ origin plane, positioning its center 12 mm down from the top face of the cylinder.

<img width="363" height="461" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/409018a9-0a7f-45d7-b806-1a887d28b968" />

10.Cut the 6 mm hole horizontally through the side wall until it breaks into the main central chamber.

<img width="503" height="605" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-03 211338" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6dd9b221-b98b-450e-a024-5463e1aa8b0d" />
<img width="281" height="386" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7ebe0960-7aa0-4120-82f9-f88141aead73" />

11.Export for 3D printing by right-clicking your body or component in the Browser tree, selecting Save As Mesh, setting the format to STL or 3MF, and saving the file.

12.Convert the 3D model into a drawing by clicking the Design workspace dropdown in the top-left corner, selecting Drawing -> From Design, choosing your sheet size, and clicking to place your base and projected views on the page.
